Selah Timer – phone app

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole "Selah App" folder onto the page.
   (Or upload the folder's contents to a GitHub Pages repo.)
2. Open the link it gives you on your phone.
3. iPhone (Safari): tap Share > "Add to Home Screen".
   Android (Chrome): tap the menu > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".

It then opens full-screen from its own icon and works offline.
